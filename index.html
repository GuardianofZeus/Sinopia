Brush tip

White paints, black is empty. PNGs with transparency use their alpha channel. You can also drop images onto the canvas.

Dynamics

Spacing and rotation apply to image tips. Round tips draw as continuous strokes.

Stroke test

Draws angles, sizes and pressure tapers with the current brush on a new layer, so you can check edges. Undo removes it.

Layers

    Double-click a name to rename it. Ctrl+click a layer or group to flash its drawing on the canvas. Drag rows to reorder: drop on a group's name to put a layer in it, or drag a group's header to move the whole group. Each group can have a Ctrl+1–9 hotkey.
